Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy explains what information Terrax SMP ("we", "us") collects when you use our Minecraft server, our website and our Discord, and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Minecraft account data: your username and UUID.
  • IP address: your IP address when you connect to the server, used for security, anti-cheat and to stop ban evasion.
  • Gameplay data: chat messages, commands, balances, homes, inventories, punishments and similar server logs.
  • Purchase data: your Minecraft username, what you bought, the amount and the order reference. Payments are handled by a third-party payment provider, so we never see or store your card number.
  • Discord data: your Discord username and ID when you join our Discord, open a ticket or buy something there.

Our website

The website does not use cookies, accounts or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts, so GitHub and Google receive your IP address and browser details when you open it, as they do for any website they serve. See their own privacy policies for details.

Why we use your data

  • To run the server and let you play.
  • To deliver and support the items you buy.
  • To keep the server safe and fair, enforce the rules and handle reports.
  • To meet legal and accounting obligations.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. We share it only with services we need to operate: our server host, our payment provider, Discord and GitHub. We may also disclose data if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Server logs are kept for a limited time and then deleted. Punishment records are kept as long as they are needed to enforce bans. Purchase records are kept for as long as accounting and tax law require.

Your rights

If you are in the European Economic Area, you can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to give you a copy of it. You can also complain to your national data protection authority. To use any of these rights, contact us on our Discord server.

Children

If you are under 16, ask a parent or guardian before using the server or buying anything.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed, and continued use of the server means you accept the current version.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us on our Discord server.
