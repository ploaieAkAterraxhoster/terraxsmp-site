About the server

Terrax SMP is a premium server. You need an official Minecraft Java Edition account to join.

The server is based on DonutSMP. You start with nothing, earn money, trade with other players and work your way up through the economy. Crates give you a way to get rare items faster, and they are opened with keys you can buy below.

Crate keys

Each key opens one crate. In game, left click a crate to open it, or right click to see what is inside before you buy.

Prices are in euros. After you order, your keys are added to your account on the server. Buying happens on our Discord, so have your Minecraft username ready.
