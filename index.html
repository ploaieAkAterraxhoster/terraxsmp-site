About the server

Terrax SMP is a premium server. You need an official Minecraft Java Edition account to join.

The server is based on DonutSMP. You start with nothing, earn money, trade with other players and work your way up through the economy. Crates give you a way to get rare items faster, and they are opened with keys you can buy below.

  • Java EditionAdd the address above in the multiplayer menu and join.
  • Premium accounts onlyCracked accounts can no longer join.
  • Player economyEarn money, sell items, buy from the shop and trade with other players.
  • Teleports and homesSend teleport requests to friends, set homes and use random teleport.
  • CratesOpen crates in game with keys. Buy keys below.
  • Fair playStaff enforce the rules, and cheaters are banned.

Found a bug, a glitch or a cheater? Report it to the staff on our Discord right away. Reports help keep the server fair for everyone.

Crate keys

Each key opens one crate. In game, left click a crate to open it, or right click to see what is inside before you buy.

Prices are in euros. Place your order on the order page and a staff member will contact you on Discord to confirm and arrange payment. Your keys are added to your account on the server once payment is confirmed. Have your Minecraft username ready.

Ranks

Every rank unlocks its own PvP kit. The higher the rank, the better the gear and the shorter the cooldown. Member is free and every player starts with it. Claim your kit in game with /kit or /kitmenu.

Prices are in euros. Place your order on the order page and a staff member will contact you on Discord to confirm and arrange payment. Have your Minecraft username ready.
